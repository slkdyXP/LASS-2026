## Prompt

> Create a publication-quality, wide four-panel scientific conceptual
> illustration for a top machine-learning paper. Pure white background,
> minimalist flat vector editorial style, crisp geometric shapes, high
> contrast, generous whitespace, consistent stroke widths, no gradients, no
> shadows, no 3D, no photorealism. Absolutely no words, no letters, no numbers,
> no typographic glyphs.
>
> Panel A: two matched social-resource vignettes side by side with the same
> visibly low shared resource level. In the left vignette, a healthy group
> surrounds a shared reservoir whose WORLD capacity mechanism/gear is damaged;
> in the right vignette, the reservoir mechanism is healthy but one identifiable
> blue participant temporarily takes extra resource because of a small emergency
> symbol. Make the observed shortage visually similar but the causes clearly
> different.
>
> Panel B: show the two distinct event streams entering an unconstrained
> reflection funnel or memory prism and collapsing into one generic dark-gray
> global belief card; use a vermilion warning/cross motif to communicate causal
> information loss without text.
>
> Panel C: show a diagnostic audit chain with four distinct icons connected
> left-to-right: observed event card, persistent memory notebook, belief
> attribution compass with three colored targets, and downstream action control
> lever. Mark the event as preserved but the attribution and action as
> problematic using purely pictorial check/warning symbols.
>
> Panel D: show an evidence gate routing colored evidence tokens into separate
> clean drawers or channels for self (green), named other (blue), shared world
> (orange), and temporal validity (purple), with a reversible rollback arrow for
> resolved evidence. Convey that correct routing preserves distinctions.
>
> Use an accessible palette inspired by Okabe-Ito: blue #0072B2, orange
> #E69F00, green #009E73, purple #CC79A7, vermilion #D55E00, neutral charcoal
> #343A40, light gray #E9ECEF. Compose as a seamless horizontal 2.3:1 figure,
> leaving blank room along the top of each panel for exact panel labels to be
> overlaid later.
